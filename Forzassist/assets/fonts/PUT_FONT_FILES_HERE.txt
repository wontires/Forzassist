Place your font files here, or in ./fonts, or next to main.py.

Recognized names include:
- Inter_18pt-Regular.ttf
- Inter_18pt-Medium.ttf
- Inter_18pt-bold.ttf
- Inter_18pt-Bold.ttf
- Inter-Regular.ttf
- Inter-Medium.ttf
- Inter-Bold.ttf

The Python launcher searches multiple locations and loads fonts before QML starts.
